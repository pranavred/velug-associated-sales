# Company platform status

The November 2026 company platform implements inventory ingestion/API integration, separate company identities and roles, live web research with constrained catalog matching, and root or `/associated-sales` deployment. The old demonstration remains available separately.

See [self-hosting](self-hosting.md), [inventory API](inventory-api.md), and [verification records](verification/).

## Remaining enterprise extensions

- Native ERP connectors beyond the generic CSV/JSON/source-token API.
- SSO/SCIM/MFA and automated email/password recovery.
- Postgres or another clustered database adapter for multi-replica self-hosting.
- Exhaustive retrieval over very large catalogs beyond the bounded lexical shortlist.
- Automated retention, export/deletion administration, and external audit integrations.
- Broader industry-specific evaluations and independent semantic verification of AI evidence.

These are extension points, not features claimed in this release. AI matches remain human-reviewed hypotheses.
